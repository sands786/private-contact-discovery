Step 01
Connect & Encrypt
Connect your Solana wallet. Contacts are hashed client-side using SHA-256 with a random salt before any data leaves your device.
SHA-256 · Salt hashing · Phantom/Backpack
Step 02
Commit On-Chain
A Merkle root of your hashed contacts is committed to a Solana session PDA. Only hashes reach Arcium — plaintext never leaves your browser.
Arcium SDK · MXE Input · Merkle commitment
Step 03
Confidential Intersection
Arcium's MXE executes PSI inside a secure enclave with session blinding. No single node ever sees the full dataset or non-matches.
Arcium MXE · PSI · Session blinding
Step 04
Reveal Only Matches
The MXE verifier posts only the intersection result back to your session account via CPI. Non-matches remain cryptographically hidden.
Verifier CPI · Solana settlement · ZK proof
