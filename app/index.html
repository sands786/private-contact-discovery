How Arcium Protected Your Privacy
🔒 What Was Hidden
Your N non-matching contacts were never transmitted in plaintext. The other party learned nothing about who else is in your address book.
⚡ MXE Guarantee
Arcium's Multi-Party Execution computed the intersection inside a cryptographically secure enclave. Even Arcium nodes cannot reconstruct individual contact lists.
⛓️ On-Chain Proof
The match count and Merkle commitment are posted to your Solana session account. Anyone can verify the computation was performed without bias.
🔑 Session Blinding
All hashes are blinded with a unique job ID before PSI runs. This prevents cross-session correlation attacks even if an adversary observes multiple sessions.
